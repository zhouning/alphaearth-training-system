# Data Availability Statement

The experiments use public benchmark datasets, including EuroSAT, BigEarthNet-S2, LandCover.ai, and LoveDA, together with a production validation corpus from Linhe County, Inner Mongolia. Public benchmark datasets are available from their original providers and are cited in the manuscript.

The reproducibility package includes experiment configuration files, summary tables, per-method result files, logs, and figure-generation scripts. The Linhe imagery cannot be redistributed in full unless permitted by the source-imagery licenses. Patch indices, processing scripts, and label-generation metadata can be shared subject to those licensing constraints. Supervisory labels in the Linhe workflow are derived from the publicly available Esri 2022 global land-use/land-cover product.

Author confirmation required before submission: repository URL, archive DOI, and exact Linhe sharing policy.
